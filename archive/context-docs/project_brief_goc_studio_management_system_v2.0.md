# Project Brief: GOC Studio Management System v2.0

## 1. Project Overview
**God of Ceramic (GOC)** is a premium automotive detailing studio based in Vadodara, Gujarat. The **GOC Studio Management System v2.0** is a comprehensive, single-tenant web application designed to digitize the entire studio lifecycle—from lead generation and interactive quotations to job execution and marketing automation.

## 2. Core Business Objectives
- **Digital Transformation:** 100% digitization of customer and lead management.
- **Operational Precision:** Automated PPF (Paint Protection Film) sq.ft tracking and inventory management.
- **Enhanced Communication:** WhatsApp-first approach for customer updates, quotations, and service reminders.
- **Financial Transparency:** GST-compliant invoicing, advance/split payment handling, and real-time revenue analytics.

## 3. High-Level Architecture
- **Frontend:** React 19 + TypeScript + Vite.
- **Backend:** Node.js + Express.js + MySQL 8.x.
- **Integrations:** MSG91 (WhatsApp Business API), Puppeteer (PDF Generation), GPS/Camera APIs (Staff Attendance).

## 4. The 11-Module Scope
1.  **Lead Management:** Multi-channel lead capture (FB/Insta/WA) and funnel tracking.
2.  **Customer & Vehicle CRM:** Centralized profiles and lifetime value (LTV) history.
3.  **Booking System:** Capacity-based scheduling and advance payment collection.
4.  **Job Card System:** The operational core tracking car status from arrival to delivery.
5.  **Quotation Tool:** Interactive SVG car diagram for visual PPF area selection.
6.  **Inventory Management:** Specialized tracking for expensive PPF rolls and consumables.
7.  **Staff Management:** GPS-based attendance with selfie verification and performance tracking.
8.  **Payment & Billing:** GST-compliant invoicing and complex payment reconciliation.
9.  **Owner Dashboard:** Real-time KPI command center for studio performance.
10. **Marketing Automation:** Triggered WhatsApp follow-ups and service reminders.
11. **Reports & Analytics:** 8+ report types with PDF/CSV export.

## 5. Visual Language
- **Theme:** GOC Premium Management (Luxury Automotive).
- **Colors:** Deep Black (#000000), Signature Red (#CC0000), Pure White (#FFFFFF).
- **Style:** High-contrast, sleek, and technical to match the premium nature of the detailing business.
