1. Executive Summary

God of Ceramic (GOC) is a premium auto detailing studio based in Vadodara, Gujarat, specializing in PPF (Paint Protection Film) installation, ceramic coating, and high-end surface protection services. This document defines the complete product requirements for the GOC Studio Management System v2.0 — a comprehensive, single-tenant SaaS-grade web application that manages the entire business lifecycle from lead capture to customer delivery and repeat follow-up.
The system replaces all manual, WhatsApp-based, and spreadsheet-driven workflows with a unified, real-time platform. It covers 11 primary functional modules including Lead CRM, Booking, Job Cards, Quotation with Drawing Tool, Inventory, Staff Management, Billing, Marketing Automation, Reporting, and Owner Dashboard.

IMPORTANT: This PRD is the single source of truth for all development, design, and QA activities. All team members, freelancers, and AI coding agents must reference this document before starting any feature development.

1.1 Business Goals
Digitize 100% of customer and lead management operations
Reduce lead response time from hours to under 5 minutes via WhatsApp automation
Eliminate manual inventory tracking errors — especially PPF roll sq.ft tracking
Enable owner to monitor studio performance in real-time from mobile or desktop
Increase repeat customer rate via automated follow-up and service reminder system
Generate professional PDF quotations with car diagram drawing tool

1.2 Target Users
User Role | Description
Studio Owner | Full access. Views dashboard, approves reports, manages staff and commissions.
Manager / Front Desk | Handles leads, bookings, job cards, billing, customer communication.
Detailer / Technician | Views assigned jobs, updates job status, marks work stages.
Sales / Follow-up Staff | Manages lead funnel, sends WhatsApp follow-ups, logs contact attempts.



2. System Overview & Architecture

The GOC Studio Management System is a full-stack single-tenant web application hosted on a cloud server. The frontend is built with React 19 + TypeScript + Vite using Stitch AI-generated components. The backend is Node.js + Express.js REST API with MySQL 8.x as the primary database.

2.1 Master Flow
The complete end-to-end business flow follows this pipeline:

1 | Lead Generated | Module 1 — Lead Management (FB / Insta / WhatsApp / Walk-in)
2 | Lead Stored in CRM | Module 1 + Module 2 — CRM auto-profile creation

4 | Quotation Created | Module 5 — Drawing-based Quotation Tool

7. Module 5 — Quotation System (Drawing Tool)

The Quotation System is one of the most unique and differentiating features of the GOC platform. It allows staff to create precise, visual quotations by selecting areas on an interactive car diagram and drawing the coverage zones for PPF or ceramic coating.

7.1 Drawing Tool — Functionality
Interactive SVG car diagram (Top view + Side view) rendered in browser
Car body parts labeled: Bonnet, Roof, Boot, Front Bumper, Rear Bumper, Doors (FL/FR/RL/RR), Pillars, Mirrors, Pillars
Click-to-select individual panels OR draw custom polygon over any area
Each selected area displays estimated sq.ft (pre-mapped per panel, per car size)
Manual sq.ft override available for custom or complex shapes
Price auto-calculated: selected sq.ft × PPF rate per sq.ft (configurable)
Multiple materials selectable per zone (3M / XPEL / SunTek — configurable)

7.2 Quotation Document Fields
Field | Description
Quotation ID | GOC-QT-XXXXXX — auto
Customer Name | Linked from CRM
Vehicle | Make, Model, Year
Date | Quotation generation date — valid for 15 days
Coverage Diagram | Annotated car image showing selected zones
Zone Breakdown | Table: Zone | Material | Sq.ft | Rate | Amount
Subtotal | Sum of all zones
Discount | Optional discount (% or fixed ₹)
GST | 18% GST toggle (optional — some customers non-GST)
Grand Total | Final amount
Terms & Conditions | Studio T&C pre-filled, editable per quotation
Validity | Valid till date (auto: quotation date + 15 days)


7.3 Quotation Output
Generate branded PDF with car diagram, zone table, and pricing
PDF includes GOC logo, red/black/white design, studio address, contact
One-tap: Send quotation PDF via WhatsApp to customer phone number
Save as template — reuse quotation configuration for same vehicle type
Link to job card if quotation is accepted and booking is created


8. Module 6 — Inventory Management

Inventory Management tracks all consumable materials used in services. Special emphasis is placed on PPF roll tracking — since PPF is sold by the sq.ft and applied from large rolls, accurate usage deduction is critical for profitability analysis.

8.1 Inventory Categories
Category | Unit | Tracking Method
PPF Rolls | sq.ft | Roll-level tracking: brand, width, total sq.ft, used sq.ft, balance sq.ft
Ceramic Coating | ml / bottle | Bottle-level: brand, size ml, quantity, used ml per job
Primer / Prep Chemicals | ml / litre | Quantity tracking — min stock alert
Car Care Products | Units / litre | Basic stock count with min alert
Microfiber / Consumables | Units | Simple count — bulk order tracking


8.2 PPF Roll — Detailed Tracking
Each PPF roll entered with: Brand, Grade, Roll Width (cm), Total Length (m) → auto-calc total sq.ft
Purchase price per sq.ft recorded for margin tracking
When PPF zone logged in job card: used sq.ft deducted from assigned roll
Multi-roll support: staff selects which roll material was cut from
Balance sq.ft displayed per roll — visual progress bar
Alert when roll balance < 10 sq.ft (configurable threshold)
Wastage field: account for cut waste per job (default: 5% — configurable)

8.3 Inventory Dashboard
Real-time stock levels for all categories
Low stock alerts — configurable threshold per item
Monthly usage report — sq.ft consumed per brand per month
Inventory valuation — current stock × purchase price
Purchase history — vendor, date, qty, price per entry

14. Database Design — MySQL 8.x (Key Tables)

The database follows a normalized relational schema with soft-delete support (deleted_at), audit timestamps (created_at, updated_at), and foreign key constraints. All monetary values stored in DECIMAL(10,2). All timestamps in UTC, displayed in IST.

Table Name | Primary Purpose
leads | All leads from all sources — funnel status, assignment, source
customers | Customer CRM profiles — lifetime value, source, connector
vehicles | Vehicles linked to customers — make, model, registration
bookings | Booking slips — date, time slot, advance, assigned staff
job_cards | Core job records — status flow, dates, assigned staff
job_services | Services per job card — type, package, price, sq.ft
job_photos | Photos linked to job cards — stage, URL, timestamp
quotations | Quotation records — diagram data (JSON), zones, pricing
quotation_zones | Individual drawing zones — part name, sq.ft, material, price
invoices | Tax invoices — linked to job card, GST details
invoice_items | Line items per invoice — description, HSN, qty, rate, tax
payments | All payment records — amount, mode, date, linked invoice
inventory_items | All inventory — name, category, unit, current stock, threshold
ppf_rolls | PPF roll-level tracking — brand, width, total sq.ft, used sq.ft
inventory_usage | Usage log — qty deducted, job card link, staff, date
staff | Staff profiles — role, salary, login credentials
attendance | GPS check-in/out records — lat/lng, photo URL, timestamp
connectors | Referral connector profiles — commission rate, bank details
connector_commissions | Commission entries per job — amount, status, paid date
whatsapp_logs | All WhatsApp messages sent — customer, template, status
notifications | In-app notification queue per user
