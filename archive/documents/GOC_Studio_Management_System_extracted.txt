MODULE 1  |  LEAD MANAGEMENT


Lead Management is the entry point of the entire system. Every potential customer — whether they inquire via Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, or simply walk into the studio — becomes a Lead. This module captures, stores, assigns, and tracks every lead through a defined funnel until they either book a service or are marked as lost.

WHAT PROBLEM DOES THIS MODULE SOLVE?


Before this system, GOC staff managed enquiries through personal WhatsApp chats and mental notes. Leads were forgotten, follow-ups were missed, and there was no way to know how many enquiries converted to actual jobs. The Lead Management module solves this completely by creating a central, searchable record of every single enquiry.

LEAD SOURCES — WHERE DO LEADS COME FROM?


The system captures leads from 5 channels automatically or manually:

Source | Capture Method | How It Works
Facebook Ads | Automatic via API | When a customer fills a Facebook Lead Ad form, the data is automatically pushed into the system via Meta Leads API webhook — no manual entry needed.
Instagram DM | Manual Entry | Staff reads the Instagram DM and manually enters the customer details into the lead form.
WhatsApp | Automatic via MSG91 | When a new customer sends a WhatsApp message to the studio number, the system automatically creates a lead record with their name and phone number.
Walk-in | Manual Entry | Front desk staff enters the customer's details using the quick lead capture form at the reception.
Reference / Connector | Manual Entry | Lead referred by a commission-based connector (car dealer, body shop, friend). Connector name is linked to the lead for commission tracking.

LEAD FUNNEL — 6 STAGES OF PROGRESS


Every lead moves through a 6-stage funnel. Each stage change is logged with the exact timestamp and the name of the staff member who made the change. This creates a complete audit trail.

Stage | Color Code | Meaning & Staff Action Required
New Lead | Blue | Lead just captured. Staff has NOT yet made contact. System sends auto-WhatsApp immediately.
Contacted | Yellow | Staff has called or messaged the customer. Awaiting customer response.
Interested | Orange | Customer responded positively. Staff should now prepare a quotation.
Quotation Sent | Purple | A PDF quotation has been generated and sent via WhatsApp to the customer.
Booked | Green | Customer confirmed the booking and paid an advance. Lead is now a Job Card.
Lost | Red / Gray | Customer declined or stopped responding. A reason must be entered before marking Lost.


RULE | No lead can be deleted from the system. Lost leads remain in the system and can be reactivated in the future.


WHATSAPP AUTO-RESPONSE FLOW


When a new lead arrives via WhatsApp or Facebook, the system takes automatic action within 5 seconds:

A welcome message with GOC's service menu is instantly sent to the customer's WhatsApp number.
A new lead record is automatically created in the CRM with the customer's name and phone.
The assigned staff member receives an in-app notification AND a WhatsApp alert on their own phone.
The system schedules automatic follow-up reminders: at +1 day, +3 days, and +7 days if no booking is confirmed.
If the customer does not respond within 24 hours, a gentle nudge message is sent automatically.

CONNECTOR / REFERRAL COMMISSION SYSTEM


A Connector is any person — a car dealer, body shop owner, or influential individual — who regularly refers customers to GOC in exchange for a commission per completed job. This sub-system tracks every referral and commission automatically.

Connector Profile | Each connector has a profile with their name, phone number, and commission rate (either a fixed rupee amount or a percentage of the job value).

Auto Commission Calculation | When a job card is marked as DELIVERED, the system automatically calculates the commission due and adds it to the connector's pending balance.
Commission Status | Each commission entry shows Pending or Paid. Owner marks it as Paid when cash/transfer is done.
Monthly Commission Report | A PDF report listing all commissions due per connector for the month, ready to download or print.

Filter by status (New / Contacted / Interested / Booked / Lost)

MODULE 3  |  BOOKING SYSTEM


The Booking System is the bridge between a confirmed lead and an active job. When a customer says 'yes' to GOC's quotation, the booking is created. It locks in the date, time slot, service, advance payment, and auto-creates the Job Card. It also prevents double bookings and sends instant WhatsApp confirmations.

WHAT PROBLEM DOES THIS MODULE SOLVE?


Earlier, bookings were managed through WhatsApp conversations and a physical diary. Slots were double-booked, advance payments were untracked, and customers received no formal confirmation. The Booking System replaces all of this with a digital calendar, payment record, and automatic confirmation message.

MODULE 4  |  JOB CARD SYSTEM — CORE ENGINE


The Job Card is the operational heart of GOC. Every single car that enters the studio — whether pre-booked or walk-in — must have a Job Card. The Job Card tracks the vehicle through every stage of work, links to photos, inventory deductions, billing, quality checks, and the final delivery. Nothing happens at GOC without a Job Card.

WHAT PROBLEM DOES THIS MODULE SOLVE?


Without a Job Card system, GOC faced constant issues: staff did not know which cars needed what work, multiple cars being serviced caused confusion about priority, QC was informal, and customers had no way to know when their car was ready. The Job Card system brings complete order, accountability, and visibility to studio operations.

JOB CARD HEADER INFORMATION

Booking Reference | Linked to the Booking ID that created this job. Walk-in jobs show 'Direct / Walk-in'.
Customer | Linked from CRM. Full name, phone, address auto-displayed.
Vehicle | Make, model, year, color, and registration number. Pulled from CRM vehicle profile.
Date In (Arrival) | Exact timestamp when the car physically arrives at the studio and is checked in.

Total Job Amount | Sum of all service charges for this job.
Total Paid | Running total of all payments received — advance + subsequent payments.
Balance Due | Total Amount minus Total Paid. Updated in real-time as payments are recorded.


JOB STATUS FLOW — 9 STAGES


Every job card moves through a fixed sequence of statuses. Each status change is time-stamped and logged with the name of the staff who changed it. This creates a full audit trail of the car's time in the studio.

Status | Code | Description & What Staff Must Do
Booked / Scheduled | SCHEDULED | Booking confirmed but car has not arrived yet. Staff should be prepared with materials and bay space.
Car Received | CAR_IN | Car physically arrived at studio. Staff must: take before-photos of full car, note any pre-existing damage, confirm service details with customer, and get customer signature on work order.
Washing / Prep | WASHING | Car is being cleaned and prepped — clay bar, chemical decontamination, drying. Must be completed before PPF or ceramic.
Main Work In Progress | IN_PROGRESS | Primary service (PPF / Ceramic / Correction) is being performed by the assigned detailer. Inventory deduction happens at this stage.
Quality Check | QC | Senior detailer inspects the completed work — checking film edges, bubbles, ceramic cure, correction depth. Must be done before customer is notified.
Rework Required | REWORK | QC failed. Specific issues logged. Job goes back to the assigned detailer for correction. Rework reason and photos must be attached.
Ready for Delivery | READY | All work passed QC. Auto-WhatsApp sent to customer: 'Your car is ready for pickup!' Balance payment due.
Delivered | DELIVERED | Car handed to customer. Full payment confirmed. Gate pass printed. After-delivery photos taken.
Cancelled | CANCELLED | Job cancelled at any stage before delivery. Reason must be entered. Advance payment handling noted.


KEY RULE | The system will NOT allow a job to move from QC_FAILED back to DELIVERED without passing through REWORK and then QC again. This enforces quality discipline.


JOB CARD — ACTIONS & FEATURES


Photo Documentation
At every stage transition, the system prompts staff to upload photos. This is mandatory at CAR_IN and DELIVERED stages.
Before photos (at CAR_IN): Full car exterior — front, rear, both sides, roof. Interior if relevant.
During photos (at IN_PROGRESS): Work progress — PPF being applied, ceramic coating spread.
After photos (at READY / DELIVERED): Final result — every panel, close-ups of work areas.
Photos stored per job card, per stage. Maximum 5MB per photo. Thumbnails auto-generated.
Customer can be shown photos at delivery as proof of work quality.

Inventory Deduction at Job Level
When the job status changes to IN_PROGRESS:
Staff selects which PPF roll or ceramic coating product was used
Enters the sq.ft of PPF applied (per zone) or ml of ceramic used
System deducts the quantity from the linked inventory item automatically
If a 5% wastage factor is configured, it is added to the deduction
This deduction is logged in the Inventory Usage report with job card reference

WhatsApp Updates to Customer
Automatic WhatsApp when status = READY: 'Your [vehicle] is ready for pickup at GOC Studio!'
Staff can manually send a status update at any stage from the job card screen
All messages sent are logged in the WhatsApp Logs with timestamp and delivery status

Print Documents from Job Card
Work Order (for technician): Job card ID, vehicle, services, notes, assigned staff — printed for bay
Gate Pass (for vehicle exit): Vehicle reg no., date, time, customer name, staff signature — printed on delivery
Invoice PDF: Fully formatted GST invoice generated in PDF from job card billing data

QUICK SERVICE MODE — WALK-IN EXPRESS


For simple, fast services (express wash, quick wax, minor polish touch-up) that do not require a full job card workflow, the Quick Service mode allows:

MODULE 5  |  QUOTATION SYSTEM WITH DRAWING TOOL


The Quotation System is GOC's most unique and visually impressive feature. It allows staff to create precise, professional quotations by physically drawing on an interactive car diagram to select which panels need PPF or ceramic coating. The result is a branded PDF quotation that customers can understand at a glance — with a car image showing highlighted zones and an itemised price breakdown.

WHAT PROBLEM DOES THIS MODULE SOLVE?


Quoting for PPF jobs was the most complicated task at GOC. Different cars have different panel sizes. Customers would ask 'how much for bonnet only?' or 'just front bumper and mirrors' — and staff had to manually calculate sq.ft and prices from memory or spreadsheets. The Drawing Tool makes this instant, accurate, and professional.

INTERACTIVE SVG CAR DRAWING TOOL


The drawing tool is a browser-based interactive SVG diagram of the vehicle. It provides:

Two view options: Top-Down View and Side View of the vehicle
All major body panels are pre-labelled and clickable: Bonnet, Roof, Front Bumper, Rear Bumper, Left Front Door, Right Front Door, Left Rear Door, Right Rear Door, Boot Lid, Left Mirror, Right Mirror, Front Pillars (A-pillars), Rear Pillars
Click on any panel to select it — selected panels highlight in GOC red color
Click again to deselect a panel
Draw custom polygon over any area for non-standard coverage zones
Each selected panel instantly shows its estimated sq.ft area — pre-mapped by car size category
Car size categories: Hatchback / Sedan / SUV / MUV / Luxury / Supercar
Manual sq.ft override: Staff can type a custom sq.ft value if the default does not match

MATERIAL & PRICING CONFIGURATION


For each selected zone, staff chooses the material to be applied:
PPF Materials: 3M Scotchgard / XPEL Ultimate Plus / SunTek Ultra — each with its own rate per sq.ft
Ceramic: Basic 7H / Premium 9H / Graphene — each with its own rate per ml/sq.ft
Rates are configured in admin settings and automatically pull into the quotation
Price auto-calculates: selected area (sq.ft) × material rate = zone price
Total across all zones is instantly updated as zones are added or removed

MODULE 6  |  INVENTORY MANAGEMENT


Inventory Management tracks every material consumed in GOC's services. The most critical and specialized part is PPF Roll Tracking — because PPF is an expensive material sold by the sq.ft and used from large rolls. Even a small error in tracking can mean thousands of rupees in unaccounted material losses. This module ensures every sq.ft of PPF is accounted for.

WHAT PROBLEM DOES THIS MODULE SOLVE?


Without inventory tracking, GOC faced material shrinkage, over-ordering, and inability to calculate true job profitability. Staff would use PPF from a roll without recording it, making it impossible to know how much material was on hand. At month-end, rolls that should have had material were empty with no explanation.

INVENTORY CATEGORIES


Category | Unit of Measurement | Tracking Detail
PPF Rolls | Square Feet (sq.ft) | Roll-level tracking — brand, grade, roll width, total sq.ft, used sq.ft, remaining sq.ft per roll
Ceramic Coating | Millilitres (ml) / Bottle | Bottle-level tracking — brand, product name, bottle size, quantity in stock, ml used per job
Primer & Prep Chemicals | ml / Litre | Quantity-based tracking with minimum stock alert
Car Care Products | Units / Litre | Basic count tracking — shampoo, polish compounds, etc.
Microfiber & Consumables | Units / Box | Simple stock count — cloths, applicators, gloves, masking tape


PPF ROLL — DETAILED ENTRY & TRACKING


When a new PPF roll is purchased and entered into the system, the following details must be filled:

Brand | 3M / XPEL / SunTek / Other (configurable list in settings)
Product Grade / Series | e.g., 3M Scotchgard Pro, XPEL Ultimate Plus, SunTek Ultra
Roll Width | Width in centimeters — e.g., 152cm, 122cm, 61cm
Roll Length | Total length in meters — e.g., 30m, 15m
Total sq.ft (Auto-calculated) | System calculates: (Width in cm × Length in m) ÷ 929 = sq.ft
Purchase Price per sq.ft | Used for material cost per job — entered at time of purchase. Critical for profitability.
Purchase Date | Date roll was received
Vendor / Supplier | Name of supplier for reorder tracking
Invoice Number | Supplier's invoice number for record-keeping
Low Stock Alert Threshold | Alert fires when remaining sq.ft drops below this number. Default: 10 sq.ft (configurable).


PPF USAGE DEDUCTION — HOW IT WORKS


Every time PPF is applied in a job, the usage must be logged. Here is the exact flow:

Staff opens the job card and navigates to the 'Inventory Used' section
Staff selects the PPF Roll used (dropdown shows all active rolls with remaining balance)
Staff enters sq.ft applied per zone (can be pulled from the quotation zones if quotation was done)
System adds configured wastage % (default 5%) to the entered sq.ft for the final deduction
Total deduction is subtracted from the selected roll's remaining balance
Usage is logged in inventory history: job card ID, staff name, date, sq.ft deducted, wastage included
If a job uses material from multiple rolls, multiple deductions can be logged

EXAMPLE | Staff applies 8 sq.ft of XPEL Ultimate from Roll #3. System deducts 8 + 5% wastage = 8.4 sq.ft from Roll #3. Roll #3 balance reduces accordingly.


INVENTORY DASHBOARD — REAL-TIME VIEW


All PPF rolls shown as cards: brand, grade, total sq.ft, used sq.ft, remaining sq.ft with visual progress bar
Ceramic and chemical inventory shown as a sortable table with quantity and status
Red alert badge on any item below its minimum threshold — visible from main navigation
Total inventory valuation: sum of (remaining qty × purchase price) for all items
Monthly usage summary: how much PPF sq.ft was used per brand this month
Purchase History tab: full log of all inventory received — date, vendor, qty, price
Reorder Suggestions: system flags items that have dropped below threshold and suggests reorder qty based on monthly consumption rate

GPS-BASED ATTENDANCE SYSTEM — DETAILED FLOW


The attendance system uses mobile GPS location combined with a mandatory selfie photograph to ensure the staff member is physically present at the studio. No separate app is needed — it works through the mobile web browser.

Check-In Process (Step by Step)
Step 1: Staff opens the GOC Management System URL on their mobile phone browser
Step 2: Staff taps 'Check In' button on the attendance screen
Step 3: Browser requests GPS location permission — staff must allow it
Step 4: System verifies that the GPS coordinates are within 50 metres of the studio's registered location
Step 5: If location verified — system opens camera for selfie. Staff takes a front-facing photo
Step 6: Selfie is submitted. System records: check-in time, GPS coordinates, selfie photo, and staff ID
Step 7: If check-in is after the configured cutoff time (default: 9:30 AM), the record is automatically marked 'Late'
Step 8: Staff sees 'Check-in recorded' confirmation on screen

Check-Out Process
Same flow — staff taps 'Check Out', GPS verified, selfie taken, checkout time recorded
Total hours worked auto-calculated from check-in to check-out time
Half-day flag if working hours below 4 hours

Attendance Failure Scenarios
GPS out of range: System shows 'You are not within studio range. Cannot check in.' — no override possible without manager intervention
Location permission denied: System shows warning and cannot proceed — staff must enable GPS on their phone
Forgot to check out: Manager can manually enter check-out time with reason logged

LEAVE MANAGEMENT


Staff submits leave request from their account: type (sick / casual / planned), dates, reason
Manager or owner receives notification and approves or rejects
Approved leaves auto-fill attendance record as 'Leave' for those dates
Monthly leave balance tracked per staff: e.g., 1 sick leave + 1 casual per month

PERFORMANCE TRACKING DASHBOARD

MODULE 8  |  PAYMENT & BILLING


The Billing module handles every financial transaction at GOC — from advance payment at booking to final settlement on delivery, including split payments, partial payments, GST invoices, proforma invoices, and refunds. All documents are GST-compliant and can be generated as PDFs instantly.

WHAT PROBLEM DOES THIS MODULE SOLVE?


GOC previously used handwritten bills and WhatsApp-based payment confirmations. There was no record of which customers had outstanding balances, advance payments were forgotten, and generating a GST-compliant tax invoice required manual computation. This module automates all of it.

PAYMENT TYPES HANDLED


Payment Type | When It Occurs | How It Works
Advance Payment | At time of booking | Amount collected at booking is recorded against the job. Auto-deducted from final invoice balance.
Milestone Payment | Mid-service (large jobs >₹15,000) | For multi-day jobs, owner can request partial payment before work is 100% complete.
Full Payment | At delivery | Complete settlement of remaining balance. Invoice auto-marked as Paid.
Split Payment | At delivery or any stage | Multiple payment modes in one transaction — e.g., ₹5,000 Cash + ₹8,000 UPI. Each mode recorded separately.
Refund | When applicable | Partial or full refund recorded against job card. Reason mandatory. Reflects in revenue reports.


PAYMENT MODES


Cash — amount entered, no reference number required
UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) — UPI transaction ID entered for record
Credit Card / Debit Card — last 4 digits and bank name noted
Bank Transfer / NEFT / RTGS — bank reference number entered
Cheque — cheque number, bank, and date noted. System flags if cheque not cleared within 3 days.

INVOICE & DOCUMENT TYPES


Document | Purpose | When Generated
Estimate | Pre-service cost estimate. Not a tax document. Sent to customer before confirming booking. | After quotation, before booking is confirmed
Proforma Invoice | Formal pre-payment document for advance collection. Looks like an invoice but marked Proforma. | At booking confirmation for advance collection
Tax Invoice (GST) | Full GST-compliant invoice with GSTIN, HSN/SAC codes, CGST+SGST breakdown. | At job completion / delivery
Non-GST Cash Bill | Simple bill without GST breakup — for walk-in customers who don't require tax invoice. | For cash customers on request
Work Order | Internal technician document — services, notes, assigned staff. Not for customer. | At job card creation
Gate Pass | Vehicle exit authorization slip — reg number, date-time, staff signature. | At delivery / car exit


TAX INVOICE — FIELD BREAKDOWN


Invoice Number | Format: GOC-INV-XXXXXX. Sequential. Resets at start of each financial year (April 1).
Invoice Date | Date of invoice generation
Customer Details | Name, address, GSTIN (if they have one for B2B jobs)
Vehicle Details | Make, model, registration number
Service Line Items | Each service as a separate line: Description | HSN/SAC Code | Quantity | Rate | Amount
Subtotal | Sum before discount and tax
Discount | Optional discount — shown as a negative line item
CGST @ 9% | Central GST — calculated on post-discount subtotal
SGST @ 9% | State GST — same rate. Total GST = 18% (within Gujarat)
IGST @ 18% | Used instead of CGST+SGST for out-of-state customers
Grand Total | Final payable amount including GST
Payment Received | Summary of all payments already received — advance + any milestones
Balance Due | Grand Total minus all payments received
Studio Details | GOC name, address, phone, GSTIN, bank account details for transfers
QR Code | Optional UPI QR code for easy payment scanning — configurable in settings


OUTSTANDING PAYMENTS TRACKING


A dedicated 'Outstanding Payments' view lists all job cards with a balance due > ₹0
Shows customer name, vehicle, job date, total amount, paid amount, and balance due
Ageing column: how many days since the car was delivered and balance remains unpaid
One-tap WhatsApp reminder: staff can send a polite payment reminder from this screen
Owner can filter by ageing (>7 days / >15 days / >30 days)

MODULE 10  |  MARKETING & FOLLOW-UP AUTOMATION


The Marketing Automation module handles all outbound customer communication — from the moment a new lead arrives to post-service follow-ups months later. All messages are sent via WhatsApp (MSG91 Business API). The system runs these communications automatically based on triggers and schedules, so staff do not need to remember to follow up.

WHAT PROBLEM DOES THIS MODULE SOLVE?


GOC's biggest challenge was the lack of follow-up after a lead or service. Staff would forget to follow up with interested customers. After a service, there was no system to remind customers to come back for maintenance. Revenue from repeat customers — which is the most profitable — was being lost simply because no one reached out at the right time.

AUTOMATED TRIGGER-BASED MESSAGES


The following messages are sent automatically by the system when specific events occur. No staff action is needed:

Trigger Event | Message Sent To | Message Content
New lead from Facebook or WhatsApp | Customer | Welcome message with GOC service menu, studio location, and operating hours
Lead status = Contacted — no reply in 24 hours | Staff + Customer | Staff: reminder to follow up. Customer: gentle nudge 'Is there any information we can help with?'
Quotation sent — no booking in 48 hours | Customer | 'We noticed you reviewed our quotation. Is there anything we can clarify or adjust for you?'
Job card status changes to READY | Customer | 'Great news! Your [Car Model] is ready for pickup at GOC Studio. Our timing: 9AM–7PM.'
Job card status changes to DELIVERED | Customer | Thank-you message + request for Google review + feedback survey link
3 months after Full Detailing service | Customer | 'Your car deserves its next detail! Book your next GOC service and keep your car looking perfect.'
6 months after Ceramic Coating service | Customer | 'Your ceramic coating is 6 months old — time for a maintenance inspection and top coat check.'
12 months after PPF installation | Customer | 'Your PPF is completing its first year! Book a free PPF inspection at GOC to ensure warranty validity.'
Customer birthday (if DOB stored) | Customer | Personalized birthday greeting with a special discount offer


WHATSAPP BROADCAST CAMPAIGNS


The owner or manager can create and send WhatsApp broadcast campaigns to any segment of customers. This is used for festive offers, new service launches, and re-engagement of inactive customers.

Audience Segmentation | Filter customers by: last service date, service type received, city/area, customer status (Active/VIP/Inactive), and lead source
Message Templates | Pre-approved WhatsApp Business message templates loaded from MSG91. Variables supported: {name}, {vehicle}, {service_date}, {discount_code}

MODULE 11  |  REPORTS & ANALYTICS


The Reports module provides the owner and management with 8 core report types that cover every dimension of the business — revenue, leads, staff performance, inventory, outstanding payments, and customer value. All reports support date range filtering and can be exported as branded PDF or Excel/CSV files.

WHAT PROBLEM DOES THIS MODULE SOLVE?


The owner had no data-driven way to evaluate the business. Questions like 'which service makes the most money?', 'which staff member is underperforming?', 'how many leads converted this month?' had no reliable answers. The Reports module answers all of these questions with accurate, real-time data extracted directly from the system.
